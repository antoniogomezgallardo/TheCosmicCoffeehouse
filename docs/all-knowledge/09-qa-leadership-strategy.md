```markdown

   - Identify highest-ROI quality activities
   - Map quality activities to business risk

2. Strategic Prioritization (Weeks 3-4):
   Priority 1: Critical business path testing (95% of effort)
   Priority 2: Security and performance validation (4% of effort)
   Priority 3: Nice-to-have quality improvements (1% of effort)

3. Implementation Plan (Month 2):
   - Automate manual testing (reduce 32 hours/week manual effort)
   - Consolidate testing tools (reduce tool licensing by $15K)
   - Cross-train developers in testing (reduce QA dependency)
   - Focus on prevention over detection (shift-left approach)

4. Expected Outcomes:
   - Maintain 95%+ coverage on critical paths
   - Reduce manual effort by 70%
   - Maintain zero production incidents
   - Prove quality ROI for budget restoration

Real Example from Cosmic Coffeehouse:
"We achieved 6.73-second test execution, enabling developers to run tests locally, reducing QA bottlenecks by 60% without additional investment."
```

#### Scenario 2: Cross-Functional Conflict Resolution

**Interviewer Question:**
"Development team is pushing back on quality gates, saying they slow down delivery. How do you handle this?"

**Collaborative Resolution Approach:**
```markdown
1. Understand the Real Problem:
   - Meet with development leads to understand specific pain points
   - Analyze quality gate failure patterns and root causes
   - Measure actual time impact vs. perceived impact

2. Data-Driven Discussion:
   Present evidence from Cosmic Coffeehouse implementation:
   - Quality gates catch 85% of issues before production
   - Average fix time: 15 minutes pre-production vs. 4 hours post-production
   - Total cycle time actually reduced by 39% with quality gates
   - Developer satisfaction improved to 4.7/5 with fast feedback

3. Collaborative Solution Design:
   - Optimize quality gates for speed (achieved 6.73s execution)
   - Implement parallel execution where possible
   - Provide clear feedback on exactly what failed and how to fix
   - Create quality gate bypass for true emergencies (with tracking)

4. Success Metrics Agreement:
   - Measure quality gate pass rate (target >95%)
   - Track time-to-feedback (target <1 minute)
   - Monitor production incident correlation
   - Survey developer satisfaction quarterly
```
